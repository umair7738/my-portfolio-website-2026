Privacy policy.

A straightforward explanation of what this portfolio does with information.

Last updated: 17 October 2026. This policy describes the current static portfolio.

1. Who operates this website

This portfolio is operated by Umair Shaikh, a full stack web developer based in Mumbai, India. Questions can be sent to umairshaikh7738@gmail.com.

2. Information you choose to share

The contact form asks for your name, email address, optional company, project type, and message. This portfolio does not store enquiries in its own database.

3. Local preferences

The site uses local browser storage to remember the selected light or dark theme and an optional reduced-motion preference. These settings stay in your browser and are not used to identify you.

4. External services

The website offers optional Google Analytics 4 measurement and Microsoft Clarity, which helps us understand how visitors interact with the site. Both load only after you choose Allow analytics. These services may process standard technical and site-interaction information; their privacy terms apply. Microsoft Privacy Statement.

5. External links

Project, LinkedIn, GitHub, CodePen, WhatsApp, email, and phone links open third-party services. This policy does not control how those services handle information.

6. Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics 4 is opt-in. If you allow analytics, the site measures page views and selected interactions such as profile downloads, project views, service and quote requests, WhatsApp, email and LinkedIn clicks, and form outcomes. Event data is limited to page paths and non-identifying interaction labels; it never includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, or message content. If you choose Not now, analytics is not loaded. To withdraw consent, clear this site's local data and reload; analytics and Clarity will then remain unloaded.

7. Data retention

This website does not keep a database of enquiries. Email providers may process or retain submitted details according to their own policies and settings.

8. Your choices

You may avoid the contact form and use email, phone, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp directly. You can clear saved theme and motion preferences through your browser's site-data controls.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's functionality or service providers change. The revision date at the top will be updated at the same time.
