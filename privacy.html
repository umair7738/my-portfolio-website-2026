Privacy policy.

A straightforward explanation of what this portfolio does with information.

Last updated: 3 August 2026. This policy describes the current static portfolio implementation and should be reviewed if analytics, a hosted form service, or other data tools are added.

1. Who operates this website

This portfolio is operated by Umair Shaikh, a frontend developer based in Mumbai, India. Questions can be sent to umairshaikh7738@gmail.com.

2. Information you choose to share

The contact form asks for your name, email address, optional company, project type, and message. In the current implementation, the form opens your email application with those details pre-filled. The website itself does not submit or store that information in a database.

3. Local preferences

The site uses local browser storage to remember the selected light or dark theme and session storage to avoid replaying the full loading animation on every page. These preferences stay in your browser and are not used to identify you.

4. External services

The website loads fonts and interface libraries from third-party content delivery networks, including Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg, and jQuery's CDN. Those providers may receive standard technical request information such as IP address, browser type, and requested file. Their own privacy terms apply.

5. External links

Project, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, email, phone, and previous-portfolio links open third-party services. This policy does not control how those services handle information.

6. Cookies and analytics

This build does not set advertising cookies and does not include analytics. If analytics or another measurement tool is added later, this policy and any required consent mechanism should be updated before activation.

7. Data retention

Because the current website does not process contact submissions through a backend, it does not keep a website database of enquiries. Emails you choose to send are retained within the relevant email services according to their settings and legal requirements.

8. Your choices

You may avoid the contact form and use email, phone, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp directly. You can clear the theme and loader preferences through your browser's site-data controls.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's functionality or service providers change. The revision date at the top will be updated at the same time.
