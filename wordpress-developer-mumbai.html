Project approach

Start with content and ownership.

01 / DISCOVERY

Define what the site must do

Share the audience, pages, content, existing brand material, and whether your team will update the site. For an existing installation, include the current URL and the problem you want solved.

02 / SCOPE

Agree on the build

Choose page templates, editing needs, integrations, and launch responsibilities before development. Plugin choices should follow the requirement, not become the requirement.

03 / DELIVERY

Build and review

Review desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, forms, and content with real examples. I can work from an existing design or help shape the frontend around a clear brief.

04 / HANDOFF

Leave a maintainable site

Check the finished pages and agree on who owns content updates, hosting access, plugin maintenance, and any follow-up support.

Is this a fit?

What to send in your first message.
